## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI has changed which skills matter in software engineering. It is unmatched at writing code, so writing code is no longer where an engineer adds the most value. What counts now is thinking like a product manager, deciding what is worth building and why, and thinking like an architect about how the pieces fit together.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
I think this shift will only go further. AI will write most of the code, and engineers will be the people who decide what gets built and how the system is designed. In ten years, a software engineer will be closer to a product manager and an architect than to a programmer.

## My hot take on AI
AI is making us lose our edge: we can't even reply to an email anymore without pasting it into ChatGPT first.
